Working memory tasks

Five short tasks. Do them in order, one at a time.

Before you start
  • Use a laptop or desktop with a keyboard (you'll need the F and J keys). No phones or tablets.
  • Find somewhere quiet and close other tabs and notifications.
  • Don't refresh or close the tab mid-task. Your data saves at the very end.
  • Take a short break between tasks if you want.

Checkmarks are saved in this browser only. If something breaks, screenshot it and message the lab.
