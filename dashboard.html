How scoring works

Complex spans (OSpan, SymSpan, RSpan, RotSpan): partial score = total items recalled in the right position, across all main sets (the usual primary DV). Absolute score = sum of set sizes for sets recalled perfectly (Unsworth et al., 2005). Perfect sets = how many sets were fully right (this is what the tasks label "absolute_span_score" in the raw file). Processing accuracy = % of main-task math / sentence / symmetry / rotation trials correct; below 85% gets flagged.
Backward digit span: span = longest length with at least 1 of 2 trials right; trials correct out of 16 main trials (the 2 practice trials are dropped, including in older files where they were mixed in).
If someone did the same task twice, the first attempt is used and the repeat is flagged. IDs like 999 or anything starting with "test" are treated as test runs.
